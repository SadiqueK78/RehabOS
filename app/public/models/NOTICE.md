# 3D coach models

## coach-senior.glb

Converted from `Male_Adult_03` in the Microsoft Rocketbox Avatar Library
(https://github.com/microsoft/Microsoft-Rocketbox). Changes: the FBX was converted to glTF
binary, bones were renamed to Mixamo names, head-top/toe-tip marker nodes were added, textures
were resized to 1024 px and the hair/eyelash opacity map was merged into the colour alpha channel.
